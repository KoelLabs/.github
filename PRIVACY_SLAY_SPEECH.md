# Slay Speech Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 11, 2026

This Slay Speech Privacy Policy explains how **Koel Labs LLC** (**Koel Labs**, **we**, **us**, or **our**) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use Slay Speech, including the Slay Speech mobile application, related websites, speech-analysis features, lessons, videos, exercises, support features, and other services that link to this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to Slay Speech. Other Koel Labs websites, code, models, public profiles, and community resources may be governed by the general Koel Labs Privacy Policy. If there is a conflict between this Slay Speech Privacy Policy and the general Koel Labs Privacy Policy, this Slay Speech Privacy Policy controls for Slay Speech.

## 1. Summary

Slay Speech helps you practice spoken English.

When you use speaking features, Slay Speech records your practice speech and sends it to Koel Labs servers so we can analyze it and return pronunciation feedback.

Unless you choose otherwise, we do **not** store your practice recordings to train our models or share them with researchers.

Slay Speech gives you two separate optional choices, both off by default:

1. **Help improve Slay Speech** — lets Koel Labs store and use your practice recordings and speech metadata to train and evaluate speech models.
2. **Contribute to speech research** — lets Koel Labs share your speech data with approved research partners under research data-use agreements.

You can change these choices in the app settings.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Speech and pronunciation data

When you use speaking features, we process information such as:

- your practice speech recordings;
- the phrase, sentence, word, lesson, video, or exercise you are practicing;
- timestamps and practice context, such as whether the recording was part of a video exercise or standalone practice;
- speech-analysis outputs;
- pronunciation feedback;
- model confidence scores or similar technical outputs;
- speech features or embeddings generated by our models, if applicable; and
- a randomly generated speaker ID used to distinguish practice sessions and evaluate model performance.

Unless you opt in to **Help improve Slay Speech** or **Contribute to speech research**, we use your practice recording to generate feedback and do not store the raw recording after the feedback request is completed.

### 3.2 Learning profile information

During onboarding and app use, we may collect information such as:

- your native language;
- your English-learning goal, such as travel, work, study, or exam practice;
- your self-assessed English speaking level;
- onboarding status;
- beta acceptance timestamp;
- app settings; and
- practice context and progress information.

Your nickname is stored locally on your device unless we tell you otherwise or you submit it to us, such as through a support screenshot.

### 3.3 App, device, and security information

We may process technical information such as:

- app version;
- operating system;
- device type;
- IP address;
- approximate region inferred from network routing;
- request timestamps;
- server logs;
- crash or diagnostic information;
- randomly generated app or speaker identifiers;
- device-integrity or app-integrity signals from Apple, Google, or similar providers; and
- information needed to detect abuse, prevent fraud, secure the Service, and confirm that requests come from a real instance of the app.

We keep Apple, Google, or similar device-integrity identifiers separate from speech metadata where feasible and use them for security and abuse-prevention purposes.

### 3.4 Notification information

If you enable notifications, we may process information such as:

- your notification preference;
- notification schedule or reminder settings; and
- push notification tokens or similar technical identifiers.

You can turn notifications off in the app settings or your device settings.

### 3.5 Feedback and support information

If you submit feedback or contact us, we may collect:

- your feedback note;
- screenshots you choose to attach;
- diagnostic information;
- contact information you provide;
- privacy request details; and
- communications with us.

Screenshots may contain personal information, depending on what is visible when you submit them.

### 3.6 Privacy choice records

We keep records of your privacy choices, such as:

- whether you opted in to model improvement;
- whether you opted in to research sharing;
- when you changed those choices;
- the version of the privacy notice or consent language shown to you; and
- records needed to process access, export, deletion, or consent-withdrawal requests.

## 6. Your Speech Data Choices

### 6.1 Help improve Slay Speech

If you turn on **Help improve Slay Speech**, you consent to Koel Labs storing and using your practice recordings and related speech metadata to train, test, evaluate, and improve Koel Labs speech models.

This may include information such as:

- practice recordings;
- target phrases;
- speech-analysis outputs;
- pronunciation feedback;
- native language;
- learning goal;
- self-assessed speaking level;
- task or lesson context;
- timestamp;
- random speaker ID; and
- consent records.

You can turn this choice off at any time in the app settings.

Turning this choice off stops future storage and use of your speech data for model improvement. It does not automatically delete speech data already stored. You may request deletion of stored cloud speech data in the app or by contacting us.

### 6.2 Contribute to speech research

If you turn on **Contribute to speech research**, you consent to Koel Labs sharing your speech data with approved research partners to support speech and language research.

Research partners must agree to restrictions that may include:

- using the data only for approved speech or language research;
- not attempting to identify you;
- not attempting to contact you;
- not using the data for advertising, employment, credit, insurance, housing, immigration, law-enforcement, eligibility, or similar decisions;
- not sharing the data onward without permission;
- using reasonable security safeguards;
- deleting or returning the data when required by the research agreement; and
- reporting security incidents involving the data.

We do not publicly release raw speech recordings unless we obtain separate, specific consent from you or unless the data has been de-identified such that it is no longer reasonably linked to you.

You can turn this choice off at any time in the app settings.

Turning this choice off stops future sharing for research. It does not automatically delete speech data already shared with approved research partners. You may request deletion of stored cloud speech data, and we will process the request as described below.

## 11. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Our current retention schedule is:

| Data category | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Practice recordings when neither optional speech-data choice is enabled | Not stored after the feedback request is completed |
| Speech data stored for model improvement | Until you withdraw consent and request deletion, until the data is no longer needed, or no later than 3 years after your last interaction with Slay Speech, unless retention is required or permitted by law |
| Speech data stored for research sharing | Until you withdraw consent and request deletion, until the data is no longer needed, or no later than 3 years after your last interaction with Slay Speech, unless retention is required or permitted by law |
| Speech features, embeddings, or derived speech data linked to your random speaker ID | Same as the speech data category from which they were derived |
| Learning profile data stored locally on your device | Until you delete local data or uninstall the app |
| Cloud learning metadata linked to optional speech-data choices | Same as the related speech data |
| Feedback notes and screenshots | Up to 24 months, unless needed longer for security, legal, or support reasons |
| Privacy request records | As long as needed to document and comply with the request and applicable law |
| Security logs and abuse-prevention records | Usually up to 90 days, unless needed longer for security, fraud prevention, legal, or compliance reasons |
| Notification tokens | Until you disable notifications, uninstall the app, or the token expires |
| Backups | Deleted or overwritten on a rolling basis, usually within 90 days |

For biometric information, to the extent applicable law treats Slay Speech data as biometric information, we destroy the information when the initial purpose for collection has been satisfied or within 3 years of your last interaction with Slay Speech, whichever occurs first, unless a different period is required or permitted by law.

De-identified or aggregated information that is no longer reasonably linked to you may be retained longer.

## 12. Deletion, Withdrawal, and Model Training Limits

You can turn off optional speech-data choices in the app settings.

Turning off **Help improve Slay Speech** stops future storage and use of your speech data for model improvement.

Turning off **Contribute to speech research** stops future sharing of your speech data with approved research partners.

You may also request deletion of cloud speech data through the app or by contacting **privacy@slayspeech.com**.

When you request deletion, we will delete or de-identify stored speech recordings and related metadata that we are legally required to delete and can reasonably identify, subject to limited exceptions such as:

- security and fraud-prevention needs;
- legal compliance;
- dispute resolution;
- backups until they expire;
- completed research records;
- completed model-training records;
- de-identified or aggregated data; and
- cases where deletion is not technically feasible or legally required.

We cannot guarantee that previously trained models can be “untrained” or that completed aggregate research results, model weights, or publications can be reversed. However, after withdrawal or deletion where applicable, we will stop using your stored speech data for future model training or future research sharing unless another lawful basis applies.
